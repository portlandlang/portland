# 0083 — `Math` is Ruby's, with domain errors as failures

- **Status:** Accepted (ruled by the deciding user 2026-11-03, accepting the recommendation in [#126](https://github.com/portlandlang/portland/issues/126); built on both implementations the same day under [#196](https://github.com/portlandlang/portland/issues/196))
- **Date:** 2026-11-03
- **Issue:** [#126](https://github.com/portlandlang/portland/issues/126)

## Context

ruby/spec `core/math/` has 29 files and 231 examples: `Math.sqrt`, `cbrt`, `sin`/`cos`/`tan` and their inverses and hyperbolics, `exp`, `log`/`log2`/`log10`, `hypot`, `atan2`, `erf`/`erfc`, `gamma`/`lgamma`, `ldexp`/`frexp`, `PI`, `E`, and `DomainError`. Portland has `Integer.sqrt` ([ADR 0058](0058-2026-10-16-builtin-types-carry-type-functions.md)) and nothing else of the family. [ADR 0021](0021-2026-07-24-namespaces-and-modules.md) already made `Math::PI` versus `Math.sqrt(4)` the house convention for a constant and a function on a namespace.

## Decision

1. **`Math` is a builtin namespace carrying Ruby's module functions, spelled as Ruby spells them**: every function in `core/math/`, taking integers or floats and answering a float, as Ruby's do. The ruby/spec files are the specification.
1. **`Math::PI` and `Math::E` are its constants** ([ADR 0053](0053-2026-09-24-constants.md)).
1. **A domain error is a failure, not a raise** ([ADR 0027](0027-2026-07-27-errors-as-typed-results.md)): `Math.sqrt(-1)` answers `failure("Numerical argument is out of domain - sqrt")`, Ruby's own sentence, so a caller unwraps it as any failure. Where Ruby answers a float — `Math.log(0)` is `-Infinity` — so does this.
1. **`include Math` refuses**, as every `include` of a non-trait does: a namespace is not a mixin ([namespaces.md](../ruby/namespaces.md)); `Math.sqrt` is the spelling.
1. **A non-number argument refuses at build time**, where Ruby raises `TypeError` at runtime; the `to_f`-coercion mock rows stay upstream.

## Consequences

- Both implementations gain the `Math` arms in the type-function dispatch and the two constants; the checker's `type_function_answer` learns they answer floats or a maybe-of-float where a domain error is possible.
- `core/math/` ports under the build issue.
- Ledger: a new [math.md](../ruby/math.md).
